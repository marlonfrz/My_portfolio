## CV and portfolio writing rules

- Use direct, natural language and avoid generic motivational claims.
- Prioritize concrete products, responsibilities, outcomes, and technical decisions.
- Emphasize end-to-end ownership, production AI integrations, automation, and product work.
- Tailor each CV to the role instead of offering one generic downloadable CV.
- Keep CV body text around 10 pt where layout permits.
- Keep logical blocks together across page breaks and avoid splitting projects unnecessarily.
- In a two-page CV, prioritize profile, experience, strengths, and key projects on page one; place stack, education, and languages on page two.
- Treat this portfolio as the broad, evergreen professional profile rather than as a replacement for role-specific applications.
